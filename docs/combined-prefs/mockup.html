Combined preferences

6 October 2026

We propose nine preferences editors in place of twenty-three. Each one gathers settings people think of together, writes the same files the OS's own editors write, and keeps the old editors in a Classic drawer. Pick an editor, switch between Simple and Advanced, and click the sections to see every page. The windows are drawn in the manner of AmigaOS 4.1, which is the look Dale asked the theme to follow.

The Prefs drawer, after

White icons are the new editors. The Classic drawer keeps every old editor, working as before, so a guide or a habit that names Prefs/ScreenMode still finds it one drawer down. Third-party editors such as Picasso96Mode and DefIcons stay where they are.

Decisions we need

1

How many windows? Nine editors, one per subject, or one Settings app holding every section.

We recommend nine: each stays small enough for a 68040 and a 256-line screen, and each icon says what it does.

2

Where do the old editors go? Into Prefs/Classic, as Dale chose for Sound, or left beside the new ones.

We recommend Prefs/Classic, the same pattern as Sound.

3

What does Simple show? One page of the most-changed settings, or the section list with fewer rows on each page.

We recommend one page, as the Sound mockup does.

4

Amiga-A for Advanced? Keep it, or move it to Amiga-Shift-A.

We recommend keeping it: no prefs editor has a Select all to clash with.

5

Which editor first? Appearance or Desktop.

We recommend Appearance: Look already exists, so it proves the shared frame with the least new code.
